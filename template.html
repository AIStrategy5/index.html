2 · How every objective must be built

Action verb (Bloom)+Condition / setting+Measurable outcome

Example: "Apply guideline-directed criteria in patients with suspected IgA nephropathy to select agents that achieve treat-to-target proteinuria reduction." One action per objective · ~3 LOs per credit hour · map to the desired Moore outcome level.

Preferred verbs by level (Bloom ↔ Moore)

  • L3 · Knowledge: recognize, describe, identify, define, list
  • L4 · Competence (preferred): apply, analyze, interpret, differentiate, integrate, justify, evaluate
  • L5 · Performance / outcomes: implement, prescribe, formulate, measure, change practice

Do NOT use — not measurable

know · understand · comprehend · appreciate · familiarize · study · be aware · become acquainted with · gain knowledge of · cover · learn · realize · perceive · think · believe

Ideal overlap

The strongest programs sit where educational need, learning objective, content, and desired outcome all overlap — write each LO so it closes a documented gap and maps to how the outcome is measured.

Weak → Strong

Weak: "Discuss the management of SLE and explain the MOA of CAR-T therapy." (weak verb, compound). Strong: "Develop a management plan for SLE patients incorporating CAR-T therapy evidence." (measurable, tied to practice change).
